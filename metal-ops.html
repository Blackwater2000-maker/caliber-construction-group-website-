    The Metal Advantage

    Built for Florida. Built to Last 50+ Years.

    Metal roofing carries a higher initial cost than asphalt — but in Florida's climate, it consistently outperforms every other roofing system on longevity, durability, and total cost of ownership.

    Hurricane Resilience

    Standing seam panels are Florida Product Approved for HVHZ — engineered to withstand the wind loads required by Miami-Dade and Florida Building Code in hurricane-prone regions.

    Energy Efficiency

    Kynar 500® light colors carry high Solar Reflectance Index (SRI) ratings, reflecting heat and reducing attic temperatures significantly — translating to meaningful AC savings year-round in Florida.

    Long-Term ROI

    A properly installed metal roof typically lasts 40–70 years with minimal maintenance. Compared to replacing an asphalt roof every 15–25 years in Florida's climate, the lifetime cost is substantially lower.
