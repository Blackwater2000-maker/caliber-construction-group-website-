Why Metal Ops

Factory-Formed Panels. Florida-Approved Performance.

Metal Ops manufactures standing seam and exposed-fastener roof panels that are tested, approved, and engineered for Florida's high-velocity hurricane zone requirements. Every panel profile carries current Florida Product Approval — including HVHZ certification for Broward and Miami-Dade counties.

Caliber Construction Group sources Metal Ops panels because the quality, consistency, and documentation standards are built for Florida's demanding code environment. When a permit requires backup, the approval sheets are ready.

  • Factory roll-formed panels with tight dimensional tolerances for clean, consistent seams on every run
  • 22, 24, and 26-gauge Galvalume steel options plus .032 and .040 aluminum
  • Copper panels available on F-15 Mechanical Seam profile for premium applications
  • 70% PVDF (Kynar 500) coating on all panels — superior fade and chalk resistance
  • Up to 40-year material and finish warranty

Finish & Color

Kynar 500® Color Palette

Every Metal Ops panel is finished with 70% PVDF — the Kynar 500 coating standard. Over 30 standard colors are available through Metal Alliance, with custom colors available on qualified orders.

Kynar 500® / 70% PVDF Finish

Kynar 500 is the benchmark fluoropolymer coating for metal roofing — formulated to resist chalking, fading, and UV degradation in Florida's intense sun. The 70% PVDF formula delivers measurably superior fade resistance compared to standard polyester or SMP finishes, with warranty coverage extending up to 40 years on qualified panels.
