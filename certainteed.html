CertainTeed Landmark®

The Standard. Refined.

CertainTeed Landmark is the original dimensional architectural shingle that set the industry benchmark — two-layer laminated construction, Class A fire rating, and a lifetime limited warranty. Twenty-one color options including CertainTeed's signature deep multidimensional blends.

For Central Florida homes, the Landmark's UL Class A fire rating and Florida Product Approval with Miami-Dade NOA coverage means it meets every code requirement — from Orlando to HVHZ-boundary counties.

CertainTeed Landmark® PRO

Maximum Definition.
Maximum Protection.

Landmark PRO takes everything that makes the Landmark great and upgrades it — Max Def color granule technology delivers dramatically deeper, more dimensional color blends that set homes apart on the street. The extended 30-year StreakFighter algae warranty means less discoloration in Florida's humid environment over time.

The SureStart protection on Landmark PRO includes labor and disposal costs during the warranty period — a meaningful real-world advantage if the roof ever requires a warranty claim.
